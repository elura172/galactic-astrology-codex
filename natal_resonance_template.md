## ✧ SYNTHESIS — The Chart's Sentence

1. the strongest measurable signatures;
2. the recurring offices across planets, gates, and chords;
3. the central tension or vow;
4. the practical offering or embodiment;
5. a concise final sentence that does not exceed what the chart supports.
