## ✧ The Two Layers: Braid and Echo

The Companion relates to the Codex through **two distinct weavings**, and the
distinction is doctrine:

**The Braid (geometric layer).** One lineage faces one face of the dodecahedron —
a one-to-one pairing of origin and passage. The braid gives each lineage a *home face*.

**The Echoes (harmonic layer).** Each lineage also sounds a *web of resonance* across
the whole mandala — glyphs from many rings, and allied bodies from beyond the 108
entirely. The lineage profiles below carry these webs. The echoes confirm what the
profiles themselves revealed: resonance does not respect the ring boundaries. The
braid is where a lineage *stands*; the echoes are where it *sings*.

**The Outer Court.** Bodies invoked by the lineages that hold no seat among the 108
are stationed in the Outer Court — true catalogue bodies, audited, standing as allied
voices around the mandala without sitting inside it. The 108 is the census of the
temple; the Outer Court is the gathering on its steps.
